# 📸 Shaurya's Photos Directory

Drop your real photos of Shaurya Chauhan and friends right into this folder!

### How to use:
1. Copy your photos into this folder:
   - `shaurya_hero.jpg`
   - `photo1.jpg`
   - `photo2.jpg`
   - `photo3.jpg`
   - etc.

2. Open `/src/data/birthdayConfig.ts`:
   - Set `url: "/photos/shaurya_hero.jpg"`
   - Customize the captions, locations, and dates!

3. Alternatively:
   - You can also use the in-browser **"Photos & Setup"** button on the website to upload images directly from your phone or laptop!
